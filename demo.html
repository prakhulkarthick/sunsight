See tomorrow's sun, today.

SunSight turns historical weather observations into next-day solar radiation forecasts for grid operators and renewable energy planners.

The problem: naive persistence forecasts say tomorrow will look exactly like today. That misses shifting weather patterns and can cause inefficient battery and grid decisions.

02 · Model performance

A linear regression model was compared against a persistence baseline that assumes tomorrow equals today.

Persistence baseline

shortwave_radiation_sum sunshine_duration temperature_2m_mean precipitation_sum

Where it excels

The model captures broad relationships among radiation, sunshine, temperature, and precipitation while beating the persistence baseline on held-out data.

Where it can fail

Linear regression assumes linear relationships and may miss complex atmospheric interactions or sudden anomalous weather shifts.
