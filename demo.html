01
Load & prepare

Load the csv file, convert Day into a datetime format, and sort records chronologically.

02
Engineer target

Shift tomorrow's radiation up with .shift(-1), so each day has a new column containing tomorrow's value.

03
Chronological split

Train on the first 80% and test on the most recent 20% to avoid future-data leakage.
